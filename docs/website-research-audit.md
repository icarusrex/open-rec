# Research corrections used for the website

The supplied Portugal report was used as a research lead, with material claims checked against primary sources on 12 October 2026. It supplied useful project leads and the 2026 amendment, but its unlinked assertions were not copied wholesale.

## Legal and operational corrections

- **Proximity:** The report's 2/10/20 km summary omits voltage levels and alternatives. Amended article 83 provides 2 km for BT, 4 km for MT, 10 km for AT and 20 km for MAT, alongside connection alternatives and low-density provisions. The website explains those qualifications rather than offering an eligibility calculator. [DL 130/2026, amended article 83](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/130-2026-1139897687)
- **CER purpose and ownership:** The report's generic non-profit description is imprecise. Amended article 189 addresses the autonomous legal entity, control, proximity, ownership, participation and primary benefit purpose. Commercial sharing projects are not automatically legal CERs. The directory preserves this uncertainty. [Amended article 189](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/130-2026-1139897687)
- **Network charges:** The report's blanket exemption for shared electricity is contradicted by ERSE's explanation of public-network sharing. Confirm current tariffs and project configuration. [ERSE self-consumption guidance](https://www.erse.pt/atividade/regulamentos-eletricidade/autoconsumo/)
- **Surplus payment:** A social tariff is not established as a universal feed-in payment. The website describes surplus sale arrangements and contract-dependent pricing without promising a guaranteed rate. [ERSE autoconsumption report](https://www.erse.pt/media/fkwdrz13/relat%C3%B3rio-sobre-o-autoconsumo.pdf)
- **Data intervals:** The half-hour/hour claim was replaced with the documented quarter-hour accounting example and EGAC sFTP guidance. [E-Redes calculation examples](https://www.e-redes.pt/sites/eredes/files/2024-08/E-REDES%20-%20Calculo%20dos%20consumos-injecao%20no%20autoconsumo%20coletivo_vfinal.pdf)
- **Procedure timing and fees:** Generic 60–90-day expectations, minimal fees, universal contract replacement and annual renewals were omitted because applicability was not established. The guide links DGEG's actual phases and project-specific requirements. [DGEG ACC/CER procedures](https://dgeg.gov.pt/pt/areas-setoriais/energia/energia-eletrica/producao-de-energia-eletrica/producao-descentralizada-autoconsumo-e-upp-mp-mn/autoconsumo-e-cer/5-autoconsumo-coletivo-e-cer/)
- **Mandated services:** A legal duty to publish a community map or voluntary contract models is not proof those resources are available. The policy record retains that distinction.

## Project and provider corrections

- **SANJOTEC:** Its own August 2026 announcement reports 271.8 kWp and operation; 312 MWh/year and the 74/26% split are estimates. The case study labels these as expected annual values, with no audited savings assertion. [SANJOTEC announcement](https://www.sanjotec.com/pt/noticias/sanjotec/sanjotec-e-cleanwatts-procuram-membros-para-comunidade-de-energia)
- **AIA:** SES's August 2025 case study reports more than 1,151 kWp, rather than the report's approximate 1 MW. Current operation, registration and benefits remain provider-reported or unknown. [SES case study](https://sesenergia.pt/pt/case-study-cer-aia-autodromo-internacional-do-algarve/)
- **Vila Verde:** CIM Cávado reports the pilot functioning, without the capacity or measured outcomes needed for an independent performance assessment. [CIM Cávado](https://www.cimcavado.pt/en/projeto-piloto-de-constituicao-de-comunidade-energetica-ja-em-funcionamento-em-vila-verde/)
- **Miranda do Douro:** Retained as a provider-reported project. The report's energy output and implied current activity were not independently established. [Cleanwatts](https://www.cleanwatts.energy/)
- **EDP:** The verified collective-sharing offer is Bairro Solar. The unsupported product label Autoconsumo Pro was replaced. [EDP Bairro Solar](https://www.edp.pt/bairro-solar/)
- **BeePlanet Energy and Wattster:** No supported identity and community-platform offering was established for the report's descriptions. They were omitted. BeePlanet Factory's official site describes BESS and energy-management products; that does not establish identity with the report's claimed UK-origin REScoop platform. [BeePlanet Factory](https://www.beeplanetfactory.com/en)
- **Pricing, platform scale and certification:** Free advisory services for all members, hundreds of operational communities, direct DGEG APIs and unnamed platform certification were not reproduced without specific evidence. UMP's proposed 400-site network was omitted as an operating community record.

## Funding and data scope

The Gulbenkian 2026 call closed on 16 September 2026. Its resource entry says closed and links the conditions and recordings. [Official call](https://gulbenkian.pt/bolsas-lista/comunidades-de-energia-gulbenkian/)

The public data model contains organisational/project information and evidence records. It excludes personal member details, meter identifiers and precise residential locations. No contract templates or coefficient spreadsheets were invented as legal or operator-ready documents. The site links official procedures and existing directories.
